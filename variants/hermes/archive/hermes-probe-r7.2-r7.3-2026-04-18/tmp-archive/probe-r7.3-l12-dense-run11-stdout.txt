The job is executing successfully, but it's hitting a **suppression rule** in the prompt. Specifically, the prompt instructs the agent to respond with `[SILENT]` if no new actionable items are found across all sections. When the agent outputs `[SILENT]`, the system suppresses the delivery entirely to avoid spamming your Discord. 

Because there's no "I checked and found nothing" notification, it looks like the job disappeared.

### The Root Cause
A conflict in the prompt's logic:
1. One rule says: *"If ALL items... are empty, you MUST respond with exactly '[SILENT]' to suppress delivery."*
2. Another rule says: *"If ALL items... are empty, your entire response is: All clear — nothing needs your attention this morning."*

The `[SILENT]` rule is winning, which kills the notification.

I will update the cron job's prompt to remove the `[SILENT]` suppression and instead force the "All clear" message. This way, you'll get a confirmation that the briefing ran, even if there's nothing new to report.
